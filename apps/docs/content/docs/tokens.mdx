Use **semantic tokens**, never raw hex or pixel values. `bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`,
`border`, `rounded-lg`. The values below come from `@plainconceptsplatform/ui-theme`.

## Color

<Palette />

The primary action color is the brand blue `#2f69ff`. The functional palette (error, warning,
success, info) each has a light tint, a solid base, and a dark variant for text on tint.

> The Special Days, Unit, Area, and Teams palettes are app-specific data-viz sets. They
> live in the app, not in the shared theme.

## Typography

The single typeface is **Outfit**. Load it with `next/font/google` and expose it as `--font-sans`.

<TypeScale />

## Radius

Radius base is `0.375rem` (~4-5px, measured from the Figma buttons and cards) with `sm`/`md`/`lg`/`xl` steps. The design uses **no shadows**.
